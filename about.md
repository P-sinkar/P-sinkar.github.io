Pranav here. This website is an attempt to put down the stuff I read, think about and try to build.

The thing I enjoy the most is learning how things work and then playing around with them. It gets reflected in:

1. Learning what incentivises humans and how it reflects in their actions.
2. Breaking down things and tech to their core: how they work, and what I can tweak to make them better.
3. Business: how it operates, what makes it what it is today, and what is the limiting factor in its next step.

Writing here is a way to think more clearly, so mostly what you will find here are unfinished notes. Each one is an attempt to be better than the last one.

CA by education, I work in the founder's office at a consumer tech startup. I hope one day the sum total of my actions will outweigh where I learned and where I worked.

I try to remind myself
                *apni aukaat ke bahar ki cheezein try kar leni chahiye, kya pata humne apni aukaat ko underestimate kiya ho.*  
<span class="translation">(Try things that seem beyond your reach. Maybe you have underestimated your reach.)</span>
