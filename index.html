Pranav here.

This website is an attempt to put down what I read, think about and try to build.

What I enjoy most is learning how things work, and then playing around with them. It shows up in three places:

  1. Understanding what incentivises people, and how that shows up in their actions.
  2. Breaking things (often tech) down to their core: how they work, and what I can tweak to make them better.
  3. Businesses: how they operate, what made them what they are today, and what limits their next step.

Writing here is a way to think more clearly, so most of what you'll find is unfinished notes. Each one is an attempt to be better than the last.

CA by education, I work in the founder's office at a consumer tech startup. I hope that one day, the sum total of my actions will outweigh where I studied and where I worked.

I try to remind myself that apni aukaat ke bahar ki cheezein try kar leni chahiye, kya pata humne apni aukaat ko underestimate kiya ho.
Try things that seem beyond your reach. Maybe you've underestimated your reach.
